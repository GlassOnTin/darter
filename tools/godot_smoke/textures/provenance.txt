# Ground textures — provenance (VISION pillar 3: CC0 sources, recorded)

All three files are DIFFUSE-COLOUR-only crops of the ambientCG 1K-JPG packs
(1024 px wide; two packs are 1024x715/737 non-square — accepted, tiling is
isotropic in both axes in the shader and the squash is not visible at the
repeats we use). Colour-space is sRGB; the loader imports them as Compressed-
Texture2D with mipmaps (see the committed .import sidecars).
